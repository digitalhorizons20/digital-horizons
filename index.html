Our Story

Many children spend hours on smartphones, gaming or watching videos. Parents worry this constant screen focus distracts kids from learning and their future. At Digital Horizons, we turn that worry into hope—children use the same screens to create instead of consume, learning coding and digital creativity that strengthens focus, builds confidence, and prepares them for tomorrow.

Our Vision

Rooted in Ethiopia, reaching everywhere: empowering kids to transform technology into creativity, innovation, and opportunity.

Our Mission

To inspire children to use technology not just for entertainment, but to create, innovate, and build their future.

Why Parents Love It

  • 🔒 Safe, structured, and engaging learning environment
  • 🌍 Affordable and accessible from home—join from Ethiopia or anywhere
  • 🎨 Screen time becomes creativity and valuable skills
