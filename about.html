About Us

Who We Are

We are a team of computer science and technology graduates with a passion for innovation and education. Some of us are parents ourselves, and we’ve experienced the worry of seeing our children spend hours glued to screens. We know firsthand how constant screen time can affect focus and creativity.

But we’ve also seen the other side: when children are given the right options, their screen time can be transformed into learning, creativity, and skill‑building. That realization inspired us to take action.

Digital Horizons was born from our desire to help families everywhere. Our initiative empowers children to use technology not just for entertainment, but as a tool to create, innovate, and prepare for the future.
